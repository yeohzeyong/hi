# Alphabet (GOOGL) — Value Check at $343.50 (October 2026)

**Verdict:** a wonderful business at a **fair** price, not a deep-value buy. **Watchlist / accumulate below ~$300.** Not the Monday buy; AON stays first.

> Not personalized financial advice. Figures come from company filings and press coverage found through web search, around Oct 3, 2026.

## The business is excellent (Q2 2026)
- Revenue **+24% to $119.8B**. Operating margin **34%** (+2 points).
- **Search +17% to $63.3B.** Google still sends ~88–91% of search referrals; AI chatbots handle only ~2–3% of search-like queries.
- **Cloud +82% to $24.8B**, with operating income of **$8.8B** (up from $2.8B a year earlier).

## But the economics have changed
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Capex | Q2 **$44.9B** (+100%); 2026 guide **$195–205B**; 2027 consensus **~$250–257B** |
| Free cash flow | **Q2 −$5.9B**, the first negative quarter in its public history. 2027 consensus is also **negative** |
| EPS | 2026 consensus **~$14.23** (+32%); 2027 **~$14.68** (**+3%**, as depreciation from the build-out hits) |
| P/E | ~24x 2026, **~23x 2027** |
| Dilution | **$80B equity raise** in 2026 (Berkshire took $10B in a private placement) |
| People | Jeff Dean and three senior AI leaders left in August (stock −4–5% that day) |
| Antitrust | April 2026 remedies: no exclusive distribution deals; must share search index and user data with qualified rivals |

## Value-investor read
- **Owner earnings are currently negative.** You are paying ~23x earnings that will barely grow in 2027, for a bet that a ~$450B two-year capex program earns high returns. It may well do so (Cloud is the proof point), but that is *betting on the outcome*, not *buying with a margin of safety*.
- **Smart money is split.** Berkshire (≈12.6% of its 13F, bought ~$15.5B in Q2) and Li Lu (~48%) are buying. But **27 superinvestors sold GOOGL in Q2 versus 4 who bought**, Ackman exited, and Fundsmith cut 40%.
- **Discount to fair value:** analyst fair value is ~$409. On my numbers, fair value is ~$380–420 → only **~10–18%** below. Compare AON at ~27–32% and SPGI at ~21%.

## Update — Oct 23, 2026 (price $324.22, Oct 22 close)
- **Price:** down 3.2% on Oct 22 in a tech sell-off driven by worries about AI capex and the cost of capital. It's ~21% below its $408 high.
- **Valuation:** ~21.5x 2027 consensus EPS ($15.07, raised in October). Reported EPS is distorted by large unrealized investment gains (Q2 reported $9.11).
- **Balance sheet:** long-term debt has doubled to ~$98B. Free cash flow is still negative. 2027 capex of $250–300B+ is possible.
- **Positives since Oct 4:** the ad-tech antitrust remedies are behavioral only (**no forced breakup**). Cloud backlog is $514B.
- **Price zones (unchanged method):** fair ~$360–400 · **buy ≤ $300** (≈20x 2027) · **strong buy ≤ $270** (≈18x).
- **Q3 earnings Oct 28 (after the close):** consensus ~$127B revenue, ~$2.9–3.0 EPS excluding gains. **2027 capex guidance** is the key swing factor.
- **Verdict:** a great business at a fair-to-slightly-cheap price. Starter position at most; the main buy is at ≤ $300.

## Update — Oct 29, 2026: why GOOGL fell ~10% after Q3 earnings
**Price:** closed $324.89 on Oct 28, then traded ~**$290–295 after hours** (−9% to −11%). Its 52-week low is $267.67.

### What went wrong (Q3 2026, reported Oct 28)
| Item | Actual | Expected | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $127.6B (+25%) | ~$128.2B | Slight miss: the **first top- and bottom-line miss in ~4 years** |
| Adjusted EPS | **$2.67** | **$3.02** | −12% miss. GAAP EPS was $2.12, including an **$8.2B unrealized loss on the SpaceX stake** |
| Search & other | $63.35B (+12%) | ~$64.9–65.0B | Miss, though query growth is the fastest in 5 years. AI Mode queries are "largely incremental" |
| Total ad revenue | $82.0B | $83.6B | Miss |
| Operating margin | 31.8% | — | Down from 34% (Q2) and 36% (Q1) as depreciation and energy costs rise |
| Free cash flow | **−$9.07B** | −$2.7B | Much worse. Includes a **$5.2B EC fine** paid in cash and a TPU inventory build |
| Capex | $51.4B in Q3; 2026 guide $195–205B kept | $55.1B | **2027 capex to "increase significantly"** (press: possibly > $300B). Details in Q4 |

### What went right
- **Google Cloud +103% to $30.7B** (vs $28.7B expected). Cloud operating income $11.1B (~36% margin). **Backlog $561B** (+$47B in the quarter). TPU system sales are contributing.

### Why the stock dropped
1. Earnings missed on the **core ads/search business**, which funds the AI build-out.
2. **Cash burn is getting worse, not better**, while 2027 spending is set to rise sharply. Investors question the return on hundreds of billions of capex.
3. Higher depreciation is squeezing margins.
4. Sentiment: AI-capex worries had already hit tech (Nasdaq −4.5% in the week of Oct 16).

### Value-investor view
- **The moat is intact:** search queries are still growing, and Cloud and TPUs are becoming a second engine. **But capital intensity has structurally risen**, so earnings power per dollar invested is lower than it was.
- Expect 2027 EPS estimates (~$15.07 before the report) to be cut ~5–8%, to **~$14.0–14.3**.
- **Revised zones:** fair value **~$340–370** (24–26x) · **buy ≤ $285–300** (~20x) · **strong buy ≤ $255–270** (~18x; near the 52-week low).
- **At ~$290 it's at the buy line, not deeply cheap.** Accumulate in thirds:
  1. 1/3 at ~$290–295.
  2. 1/3 at ≤ $275.
  3. 1/3 at ≤ $260, or after the Q4 report (early Feb 2027) gives the 2027 capex number.
- **Thesis-break watch:** search revenue growth < 5%, Cloud margins falling back, or 2027 capex > $300B without matching Cloud backlog growth.
