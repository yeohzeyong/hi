## How to buy it ($1,000 this month)
- **$500 now** (limit ≤ $430, about 1 share).
- **$500 after the Q3 report (expected Oct 27; one source says Oct 22, so check SPGI's investor relations page)**, or earlier if it dips to ≤ $415.
  - Consensus Q3 adjusted EPS is ~$4.42–4.52, a reported decline because of the Mobility spin-off. Don't panic on that headline.
- **Every month after that:** keep adding $1,000, and add more whenever it's ≤ $400.

## When to sell (thesis breaks)
- Regulators remove the legal role of credit ratings, or ban the issuer-pays model.
- Index-licensing fees collapse (e.g., the S&P 500 loses benchmark status).
- AI demonstrably erodes **Ratings or Indices** revenue, not just Market Intelligence.
- A 2008-scale reputational scandal.

Otherwise, hold for decades.
