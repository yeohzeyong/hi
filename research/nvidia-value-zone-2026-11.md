# Nvidia (NVDA) — Value Buy Zone, Nov 12, 2026

> Not investment advice. Scenario values are my estimates. Prices: $173.73 close on Nov 11, then **~$157–160 after hours** on the WSJ report that the board is preparing to oust CEO Jensen Huang.

## What's happening
- **CEO crisis:** a board investigation into Huang's affairs, including one with a **suspected Chinese spy** and one with a direct-report VP. WSJ reports the board may oust him; Nvidia is exploring replacements. Roughly **$600B of market value** was lost this week. Not confirmed yet.
- **Analysts:**
  - Baird downgraded to Neutral and cut its target **$500 → $200**, citing CEO risk, AI capex possibly only +40% in 2027, H200 China export risk and a possible $5B inventory write-down.
  - Aletheia downgraded to Hold. Morgan Stanley removed it from its top picks ($300 target kept). Goldman kept Buy at $300.
  - Median target ~$314.
- **Earnings: Nov 17 (after the close).** Consensus Q3 EPS ~$2.37–2.53, revenue ~$104–111B.
- **Consensus EPS:** FY2027 (ends Jan 2027) **$9.29** · FY2028 **$15.81** (+70%).

## Valuation at ~$160
| Measure | Multiple |

## How to act
1. **Wait for Nov 17 earnings and the board's decision on Huang.** Both could move the stock ±10–15%.
2. Then buy in thirds:
   - 1/3 at **$150–160**.
   - 1/3 at **$135–145**.
   - 1/3 once there's clarity (a new-CEO plan, or guidance confirming 2027 demand).
3. **Size small: ≤ 5–10% of the portfolio.** You already hold MSFT and GOOG, which are Nvidia's biggest customers, so this adds to the same AI-capex bet.
4. **Thesis breaks:** hyperscaler capex guidance cut, gross margin < 65%, or a key customer (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium, Microsoft Maia) shifting away from Nvidia at scale.
