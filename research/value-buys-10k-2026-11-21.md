## Correction after a critical review: the third pick is TSMC, not UNH

1. **The threat hits its core profit engine.** The CRUSH proposal audits Medicare Advantage risk coding, which is exactly where UNH's MA/Optum profits come from. DOJ civil and criminal probes into its coding are still open. Kaiser's record settlement in Jan 2026 shows the enforcement direction. The "$20.50 run rate" is the very number in question, so the 17x P/E may be on overstated earnings.
2. **It's binary, and the answer is close.** Comments close Jan 19, 2027, so waiting costs little. The stock is still falling: −15% in two days with no stabilization, which fails our trend test.
3. **Its moat is weakening.** The 2025 collapse, the Jan 2026 −19.6% day and now this. That points to regulatory dependence, the "too hard" pile.

→ **Watchlist only.** Revisit after the rule is final or 2027 guidance is out.
