## 3. New ideas in detail
### Marsh (MRSH): the lower-risk way to own the broker thesis
- **Moat:** the world's largest insurance broker and risk adviser (Marsh, Guy Carpenter, Mercer, Oliver Wyman). Same oligopoly and switching-cost economics as AON, with no underwriting risk.
- **Q3 2026 (Oct 15):** revenue $6.86B (+8% reported, +5% underlying), adjusted EPS **$2.03 (+10%)**, beating ~$1.96–2.01 estimates. Consulting outpaced Risk & Insurance (+3%), and reinsurance declined.
- **Valuation:** ~17x 2026 EPS (consensus ~$10.43) vs a usual low-to-mid 20s. Broker valuations are at decade lows because of the soft market and AI fears.
- **AON vs Marsh:**

| | AON | Marsh |
|---|---|---|
| Cheapness | AON is cheaper (~14.5x vs ~17x) | |
| Balance sheet | Takes debt to ~4.8x for USI; buybacks paused | Cleaner balance sheet, still buying back stock |
| Expected return | Higher, but riskier | Lower, but steadier |

- **Owning both?** That doubles up on the soft insurance cycle. Pick one as core, or split the "broker" slot.

### RELX: SPGI's thesis, but cheaper
- **Moat:** LexisNexis (legal research and risk), Elsevier (science journals and ScienceDirect), Risk & Business Analytics. Subscription data, deeply embedded in workflows. Long-time core holding of quality investors such as Nick Train (Lindsell Train). Morningstar rates it a wide moat.
- **Why cheap:** an AI contract-review and legal tool launch hit legal and data stocks. Shares fell about **50% from the 2025 peak** (Morningstar). The worry is that AI erodes subscription pricing power.
- **Counterpoint:** management says AI *speeds up* its product launches while keeping cost growth below revenue growth. FY2025 adjusted EPS was 128.5p (+10% at constant currency), with "strong growth" guided for 2026. Buybacks continue. Bank of America reaffirmed Buy (4,000p target) on Oct 15.
- **Valuation (estimates):** ~18–20x forward vs ~25–30x historically. Morningstar fair value $57 vs ~$37 ADR. My conservative fair value is ~$50–52.
- **Risks:** a real AI threat to legal research pricing; GBP currency exposure; a UK listing (the US ADR is liquid).
