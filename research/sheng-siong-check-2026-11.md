# Sheng Siong Group (SGX: OV8) — Check, Nov 3, 2026

> Not investment advice. FY2026 and FY2027 figures are my estimates. Price ~S$2.42 (Nov 2 intraday); quotes vary by source.

## What happened
- **3Q FY2026 (Oct 29):** revenue S$441.5M (+6.2%), net profit S$44.6M (only +1.8%).
  - Gross margin rose to 32.2% (+0.7 pts).
  - But **same-store sales fell 0.5%**, distribution costs rose 11.4%, admin costs 12.1% (wages, new-store headcount, lease depreciation). Store closures cut revenue growth by ~2 pts.
  - 9M operating cash flow fell 31% (vendor timing).
- **Outlook:** management warned of persistent discounting and Progressive Wage Model / NWC wage increases into 2027. Singapore unemployment ticked up to 2.0%.
- **Stock:** −5% on Oct 30, **−9% to −9.7% on Nov 2** (low ~S$2.42). Down ~17% in a month and ~20% over 90 days.
- **Brokers:** PhillipCapital cut its target to S$2.75 (from S$3.31). DBS Hold, S$3.00. OCBC Hold, S$2.89. RHB Buy, S$3.59. Consensus ~S$3.19.

## Moat and quality
- **Narrow moat:** #2 grocer in Singapore behind NTUC FairPrice. HDB heartland locations, its own Mandai distribution centre, a low-cost operation.
- **High quality:** ROE ~25%+, **net cash, no debt**, 70% payout.
- **Limits:** Singapore-only growth (92 stores) that depends on winning HDB tenders; intense discounting by FairPrice, DFI and online grocers; rising wages.
- **Score: ~72/100.** Moat 7 · safety 8.5 · value 6.5 · growth 5 · management 8.

## Verdict
**A good defensive dividend stock, but not yet a value buy at S$2.42.**
- Expected return ≈ 4.4% earnings yield + ~3–5% growth ≈ **~8–9% a year**. That's below SPGI, RELX and GOOG at current prices.
- **Accumulate at ≤ S$2.20.** Add more at ≤ S$2.00.
