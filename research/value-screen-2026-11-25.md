- **Alphabet Q3 (Oct 28) was its first miss on both revenue and EPS in about four years:**

  - **Free cash flow was −$9.1B.**
  - Capex is ~$200B in 2026 and expected **above $300B in 2027**.
- **AI-platform owner earnings are shrinking.** Alphabet, Meta, Microsoft and Amazon are all spending heavily on AI. Capital-light moats now deserve a valuation premium.
