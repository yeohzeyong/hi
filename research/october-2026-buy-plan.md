## Invest now or wait?
**Invest now, but in two halves around earnings.**
- **Don't wait for a market crash.** With $1,000 a month, buying every month *is* your timing protection. Waiting usually costs more than it saves.
- **Be selective about *what* you buy.** The overall market is expensive: the CAPE is ~40 and the 10-year Treasury yields ~5.2%. Only buy stocks that sit **below fair value**. Skip the ones that have rallied (Visa, Mastercard, Moody's, Elevance, Microsoft).
- **Split around earnings.** Four of the picks report in the next two weeks. Buy about half now, and the rest after each report confirms the thesis (or gives a lower price).
