## Why SPGI wins for this goal
1. **More reliable earnings.** About two-thirds of revenue and over 80% of operating profit come from "benchmark" products: ratings, indices and Platts. These are hard to replace and require no lending.
2. **The disruption fear is on its smallest segment.** In Q2 2026 Ratings revenue grew 17% and Indices 20%. The AI worry is about Market Intelligence, the lowest-margin segment.
3. **Still a discount.** At ~19x 2027 earnings it is far below its usual 25–30x, while growing EPS ~10–12%. That's a wonderful business at a fair-to-cheap price.
4. **The company is shrinking its own share count**: a $7B buyback after the Mobility spin-off.

## When AXP would be the better pick
- If you want **maximum statistical cheapness** and can tolerate deeper drops in a recession. At ~15x 2027 EPS it is the cheaper stock and could compound slightly faster *if* the credit cycle stays benign.
- It's a fine **second** purchase next month, ideally after its Oct 23 earnings confirm write-offs are stable.

## Correction to earlier figures
Earlier reports quoted SPGI at "~19.8x 2026 EPS ($19.40–19.65 guide)". That guidance was rebased after the **Mobility spin-off on Jul 1, 2026**. The correct comparison basis is **~19x 2027 consensus EPS (~$20.2–20.8)**. The conclusion and fair value (~$490) are unchanged.
